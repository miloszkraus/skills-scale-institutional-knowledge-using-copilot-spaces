# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define quality strategy, coordinate test coverage, and verify that projects meet acceptance criteria before release. They work closely with developers and product owners to ensure release readiness and customer confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA approach and test strategy for each project
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Identify quality gaps, defects, and risks that need follow-up
- Support release sign-off and post-deployment verification

### Goals
- Deliver high-confidence releases with fewer defects in production
- Improve test coverage and reduce escaped issues
- Support consistent quality standards across projects

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA sync with PM and engineering leads
- Acceptance criteria review sessions
- Defect triage and release readiness discussions
- Status updates during release and deployment planning

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate feature correctness and fix regressions.
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are measurable and testable.
- Coordinates with the Project Manager to align test planning with project milestones and release dates.

---

## Security Lead / Officer

### Role Summary
Security Leads protect OctoAcme projects by embedding security requirements, reviewing risks, and guiding incident response. They help the team make secure decisions without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs, architecture, and code for security issues
- Define security requirements and guardrails for projects
- Lead security testing, scanning, and risk assessments
- Coordinate security incident response and remediation
- Educate teams on secure development practices

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and exposure
- Maintain stakeholder trust and compliance expectations
- Allow delivery teams to move quickly while staying secure

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews during planning and implementation
- Escalation updates for security issues and incidents
- Security guidance in architecture and release discussions
- Incident postmortems and mitigation follow-up

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers and Tech Leads on secure design patterns and review findings.
- Supports Project Managers and Sponsors on risk escalation and incident communication.
- Works with Product Managers to balance business goals with security constraints and compliance needs.

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide the business context, direction, and approval needed for a project to move forward. They represent customer needs, organizational priorities, and funding decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic priorities and business objectives
- Approve project charter, scope changes, and major milestones
- Provide guidance on trade-offs, priorities, and dependencies
- Help resolve resource constraints and organizational blockers
- Support communication and decision-making across stakeholder groups

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and strategic alignment
- Maintain visibility into progress, outcomes, and risks
- Enable smooth decision-making at the executive or sponsor level

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Executive reviews and approval checkpoints
- Escalation communication for high-impact risks or blockers
- Business outcome reviews after release or launch

### Interaction with existing roles
- Gives Product Managers direction on goals and business outcomes.
- Works with Project Managers to align priorities, risk exposure, and decisions.
- Collaborates with Security and QA leads when issues affect compliance, quality, or release readiness.

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads define technical direction, guide solution design, and help ensure the architecture remains robust, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and major design patterns
- Review technical proposals and design docs
- Mentor developers and shape engineering standards
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Support technical debt reduction and long-term maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, reliable solutions with clear technical ownership
- Maintain consistency and quality across engineering work
- Reduce long-term risk and complexity in the system design

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and technical design reviews
- Code review alignment and mentorship discussions
- Technical risk and dependency discussions with PMs and stakeholders
- Architecture decision records and technical trade-off documentation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Guides Developers on implementation choices and technical best practices.
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers on trade-offs between speed, scope, and technical health.
- Provides input to Security and QA leads on architecture risk, testability, and rollout readiness.

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help the team work effectively, improve delivery flow, and sustain healthy practices across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies and team collaboration routines
- Remove or escalate blockers that slow delivery
- Support team improvement through retrospectives and feedback loops
- Help the team maintain focus on priorities and well-defined scope
- Coach teams on agile practices, communication, and continuous improvement

### Goals
- Improve team flow, predictability, and morale
- Reduce friction and unnecessary process overhead
- Support sustainable, high-performing delivery practices

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospectives and team health discussions
- Coaching sessions and blocker management updates
- Metrics and improvement discussions with PMs and Product Leads

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers and Project Managers to improve cadence and reduce blockers.
- Partners with Product Managers to support prioritization and clearer backlog health.
- Supports the broader team in maintaining a healthy, psychologically safe working environment.

---

## How these personas work together
These roles complement the existing core personas in OctoAcme. Product Managers define customer value and outcome priorities, while Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedule, and communication. Developers build the solution, QA / Testing Leads verify quality, Security Leads manage risk exposure, Tech Leads maintain architecture health, and Stakeholders / Sponsors provide direction and approval. Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches help the team operate effectively and continuously improve. Together, these roles create clearer accountability and more predictable project outcomes.
