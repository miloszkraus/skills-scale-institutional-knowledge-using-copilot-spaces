# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation Hub

## Overview

OctoAcme runs cross-functional projects using a structured, customer-first delivery model that moves from idea validation to planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. The team emphasizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and psychological safety so projects remain aligned to customer value while balancing speed, risk, and quality. Each initiative begins with a lightweight definition of the problem, stakeholders, and success metrics, then grows into a backlog, timeline, and delivery plan with explicit roles and accountability.

The process is designed to keep work visible and manageable across the full project life cycle. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers build and validate the work; and QA ensures quality and acceptance criteria are met. Communication is intentionally regular and role-based, combining daily team updates, weekly syncs, milestone reviews, and escalation paths for blockers or incidents. Quality assurance is embedded throughout delivery, with CI checks, testing standards, release gates, and retrospectives that convert lessons learned into improvements.

## Project Lifecycle at a Glance

1. Initiation — validate the business need, align stakeholders, and develop a one-pager
2. Planning — break work into deliverable increments, estimate scope, and identify dependencies
3. Execution — deliver iteratively, track progress, manage blockers, and monitor metrics
4. Release — deploy with checks, rollback plans, and stakeholder communication
5. Retrospective — capture learnings and convert them into action items for improvement

## How to Use These Documents

- Start with the [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md) to understand the overall program model and common terminology.
- For new work or a new project idea, use the [Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md) to validate the initiative and align stakeholders.
- When the work has been approved, move to [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) to scope the effort, define the backlog, and establish milestones.
- During delivery, reference [Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) and [Risk Management & Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) to monitor progress and handle issues.
- Before shipping, follow the [Release & Deployment Guide](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) to confirm quality gates, release readiness, and rollback plans.
- After each sprint or milestone, run a [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) to capture learning and drive better execution.
